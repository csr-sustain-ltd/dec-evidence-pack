CSR Sustain has prepared the information needed for your Display Energy Certificate. Please check it, complete the details requested and sign. It takes about five minutes.

1

Check the energy data

Monthly figures from your utility bills for the period shown. If anything looks wrong, say so below, or contact CSR Sustain before signing.

If your building uses a biomass fuel, please state the biomass fuel type and the calorific value (obtain this from your fuel provider) below.

2

Confirm your opening hours

Please confirm your published opening hours when the building is fully open to the public. In the absence of published opening hours, please confirm operational/opening hours when the building occupancy exceeds 25% of normal maximum occupancy/capacity.

3

Changes since the last DEC

For your DEC renewal, please confirm whether the building has undergone any material change since the last Display Energy Certificate assessment. Material change includes:

  • The addition of a building or buildings
  • The addition of an extension
  • The refurbishment of any part of the building
  • The upgrade or replacement of any building services plant (e.g. boiler)
  • The switching of a fuel source (e.g. from oil to gas)
  • The installation or replacement of energy meters
  • The addition of any renewable energy sources
