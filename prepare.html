Monthly consumption

The client will be asked to enter the monthly figures from their utility bills for the consumption period set above, including any fuel oil, biomass and renewables generation.

Tick all that apply. The client confirms the data and its source when they sign.

Monthly totals, not meter readings. Months well above the typical month are highlighted so you can check them before sending.

Tip: you can copy figures from Excel or an exported spreadsheet and paste them straight in. Click the first cell to fill (for example July electricity) and press Ctrl+V (or ⌘+V on a Mac). Whole columns or blocks are filled in one go; month labels and headings are ignored.
